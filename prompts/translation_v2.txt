ORTHOGRAPHIC RULES:
- Use ک (U+06A9) not ك (U+0643)
- Use ی (U+06CC) not ي (U+064A) or ى (U+0649)
- Use ە (U+06D5) for word-final ae sound
- Remove tatweel ـ (U+0640)
- Use ، (Arabic comma U+060C) not , (Latin comma)
- Use ؟ (Arabic question mark U+061F) not ? (Latin question mark)
- Use ؛ (Arabic semicolon U+061B) not ; (Latin semicolon)
